## 6. How to spend the $100

**The approach:** spend a little on video views first to build an audience, then spend most of it on **message ads (Click-to-WhatsApp and Instagram Direct)**. In Kurdistan, people would much rather chat than fill in a form, so messages turn into sales best.

| Campaign                        | Dates        | Objective                                               | Budget                       | Creatives                       |
| ------------------------------- | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **A: Warm-up**                  | 1 to 6 Dec   | Boost Reels (ThruPlay)                                  | **$10** ($5 + $5)            | Posts 1 and 3                   |
| **B1: Small businesses**        | 7 to 30 Dec  | Engagement → **Messages** (WhatsApp + Instagram Direct) | **$48** ($2/day × 24 days)   | Posts 5, 6, 8, 12, rotated      |
| **B2: Bigger businesses (Pro)** | 7 to 30 Dec  | Engagement → **Messages**                               | **$24** ($1/day × 24 days)   | Posts 5, 9, 11, 14, 15, rotated |
| **C: Year-end push**            | 21 to 30 Dec | Added to B1 (Messages)                                  | **$18** (about $2/day extra) | Posts 16 and 18                 |
| **Total**                       |              |                                                         | **$100**                     |                                 |

Why only $24 for the big tiers: one $1,500 to $5,000 deal is worth more than ten small sites, but big buyers decide slowly and need trust first. In month one, the Pro ad set's job is to start conversations and book consultations, not to close on the spot.

**Targeting (B1, small businesses):**

- **Locations:** Erbil, Sulaymaniyah, Duhok (+ 25 km radius).
- **Age:** 22 to 55. **Languages:** Kurdish and Arabic.
- **Audience:** Advantage+ audience, with suggestions for "Small business owners", "Facebook Page admins", "Restaurants", "Beauty salons" and "Entrepreneurship".
- **Retargeting:** people who watched 50% or more of Posts 1 or 3, plus everyone who engaged with @beeragencyy in the last 60 days. Give it about $0.50 of the $2/day.

**Targeting (B2, bigger businesses):**

- **Locations:** the same three cities. **Age:** 28 to 55.
- **Audience:** Advantage+ audience, with suggestions for "Business owners", "Entrepreneurship", "E-commerce", "Online shopping", "Real estate", "Hotels" and "Clinics / healthcare".
- **Greeting** (separate from B1, so you know which leads are big ones):
  > سڵاو! 👋 پڕۆژەیەکی گەورەترت لە بیرە، وەک فرۆشگای ئۆنلاین یان سیستەمێکی تایبەت؟ کەمێک باسی بکە.

  ("Hi! Do you have a bigger project in mind, like an online store or a custom system? Tell us a little about it.")

**For both:**

- **Placements:** Instagram Feed, Reels and Stories, plus Facebook Feed. Turn off Audience Network.
- **B1 greeting message in WhatsApp** (Message template in Ads Manager):
  > سڵاو! 👋 دەتەوێت ماڵپەڕێک بۆ بزنسەکەت؟ بزنسەکەت چییە و لە کوێیە؟

  ("Hi! Do you want a website for your business? What is your business and where is it?")

**Rules for adjusting:**

- Check Ads Manager **every 3 days**.
- Once a creative has spent **$5**, pause it if a conversation costs more than **$2** (B1) or **$4** (B2), and move its budget to the best one.
- Never edit an ad that's working. Duplicate it instead.
- If conversations are cheap but nobody buys, the problem is the reply script (section 7), not the ads.

**Rough expectations.** These are targets to measure against, not guarantees.

- **Small businesses (B1 + C, $66):** about **40 to 100 conversations**, **10 to 25 qualified leads**, and **3 to 6 small websites** sold. That's roughly **$500 to $2,000**.
- **Bigger businesses (B2, $24):** about **5 to 20 conversations** and **2 to 5 consultations**. **0 to 1 deal** may close in December, worth **$800 to $5,000**. Others usually close in January or February, so keep following up.
- **Altogether:** about **$500 to $2,000** from small sites in the month, plus the chance of one large project, all from $100 of ads.

## 7. WhatsApp reply script (this decides the sales)

Reply within **15 minutes** between 10:00 and 22:00. Speed matters more than anything else here.

1. **Greet and qualify:**
   > سڵاو [ناو]! 🙌 بزنسەکەت چییە و لە کام شارە؟ ماڵپەڕت پێشتر هەبووە؟

   ("Hi [name]! What is your business and which city is it in? Have you had a website before?")
2. **Work out which tier fits** from their answer. Do they only need to be found and called? Do they want to edit it themselves? Are they selling online? Do they run staff or branches?

**Small projects (tiers 1 to 3):**

3. Send the matching package as a single image, plus one demo link.
4. Ask three questions: how many pages, which languages, and the deadline.
5. Give a fixed price and date, for example "$250, live on 18 December", and ask for a 50% deposit.

**Big projects (tiers 4 to 6):**

3. Don't give a price in chat. Send the "from" price and the beer.krd demo video, then book a **free 30-minute consultation**, in person in Erbil or on a video call:
   > بۆ ئەوەی نرخێکی دروستت پێبدەین، با ٣٠ خولەک قسە بکەین. کام ڕۆژ بۆت گونجاوە؟

   ("To give you an accurate price, let's talk for 30 minutes. Which day suits you?")
4. In the meeting, find out the goal, the users, must-have features, the deadline and the budget range.
5. Within **48 hours**, send a 1 to 2 page proposal: scope, a timeline with previews, price in milestones (40/40/20), and a support plan.
6. Follow up on day 3 and day 7. Big decisions take time; one polite follow-up a week is fine.

**Everyone:** if there's no reply after 24 hours, send one follow-up with a demo video. After that, stop (big projects: see step 6).

Log every serious conversation as a lead in **admin.beer.krd → Leads** (status: new → contacted → proposal → won/lost). At the end of the month you'll then see exactly what the $100 produced.

## 9. The posts themselves

All 20 posts are generated from [`marketing/posts/src/posts.ts`](posts/src/posts.ts): the slide text, captions and Reel scripts. Package prices are read from the website's pricing content, so the posts and the site always match.

To regenerate, run this with the local web and admin dev servers running (they provide the beer.krd screenshots):

```bash
pnpm --filter @beer/posts render
```

It writes to `marketing/posts/out/`:

- `NN-slug/`: the images for each post: carousel slides, single posts (4:5), and Reel covers and Stories (9:16);
- `captions.md`: every caption ready to copy and paste, plus the Reel shooting scripts;
- `index.html`: a review gallery of everything.
