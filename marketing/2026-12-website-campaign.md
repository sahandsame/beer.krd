## 6. How the $100 works (automatic promotion)

You've already set **$100 for the month** to promote the newest post automatically. Each new post takes over as the ad until the next one goes up.

- **About $3.30 a day, roughly $5 per post.** With "post, post, rest", the **first post of each pair runs about 1 day and the second about 2 days**, because nothing replaces it on the rest day. That's why the stronger post of each pair is placed second, for example the packages carousel (post 4) and the domain post (post 10).
- **Check the promotion settings once**, before 3 December:
  - **Goal:** messages (WhatsApp or Instagram Direct) if it's offered. Otherwise website visits to `beer.krd/ckb/pricing`.
  - **Audience:** Erbil, Sulaymaniyah and Duhok, ages 22 to 55, Kurdish and Arabic. Or the automatic audience, if the results are good.
  - **Placements:** Instagram Feed, Reels and Stories, plus Facebook Feed.
  - **Greeting message** (if messages are the goal):
    > سڵاو! 👋 دەتەوێت ماڵپەڕێک بۆ بزنسەکەت؟ بزنسەکەت چییە و لە کوێیە؟

    ("Hi! Do you want a website for your business? What is your business and where is it?")
- **Don't post anything unplanned** (memes, reposts) during the month. It would take over the ad budget.
- **Check the results every 3 days** in Meta Business Suite: reach, messages and cost per message. If one kind of post clearly does better (for example Reels with prices), use more of it in January.

**Rough expectations.** These are targets to measure against, not guarantees.

- **Small businesses:** about **40 to 100 conversations**, **10 to 25 serious leads**, and **3 to 6 small websites** sold. That's roughly **$500 to $2,000**.
- **Bigger businesses:** **2 to 5 consultations**. **0 to 1 deal** may close in December, worth **$800 to $5,000**. Others usually close in January or February, so keep following up.

## 7. WhatsApp reply script (this decides the sales)

Reply within **15 minutes** between 10:00 and 22:00. Speed matters more than anything else here.

1. **Greet and qualify:**
   > سڵاو [ناو]! 🙌 بزنسەکەت چییە و لە کام شارە؟ ماڵپەڕت پێشتر هەبووە؟

   ("Hi [name]! What is your business and which city is it in? Have you had a website before?")
2. **Work out which tier fits** from their answer. Do they only need to be found and called? Do they want to edit it themselves? Are they selling online? Do they run staff or branches?

**Small projects (tiers 1 to 3):**

3. Send the matching package as a single image, plus one demo link.
4. Ask three questions: how many pages, which languages, and the deadline.
5. Give a fixed price and date, for example "$250, live on 18 December", and ask for a 50% deposit.

**Big projects (tiers 4 to 6):**

3. Don't give a price in chat. Send the "from" price and the beer.krd demo video, then book a **free 30-minute consultation**, in person in Erbil or on a video call:
   > بۆ ئەوەی نرخێکی دروستت پێبدەین، با ٣٠ خولەک قسە بکەین. کام ڕۆژ بۆت گونجاوە؟

   ("To give you an accurate price, let's talk for 30 minutes. Which day suits you?")
4. In the meeting, find out the goal, the users, must-have features, the deadline and the budget range.
5. Within **48 hours**, send a 1 to 2 page proposal: scope, a timeline with previews, price in milestones (40/40/20), and a support plan.
6. Follow up on day 3 and day 7. Big decisions take time; one polite follow-up a week is fine.

**Everyone:** if there's no reply after 24 hours, send one follow-up with a demo video. After that, stop (big projects: see step 6).

Log every serious conversation as a lead in **admin.beer.krd → Leads** (status: new → contacted → proposal → won/lost). At the end of the month you'll then see exactly what the $100 produced.

## 9. The posts themselves

All 20 posts are generated from [`marketing/posts/src/posts.ts`](posts/src/posts.ts): the slide text, captions and Reel scripts. Package prices are read from the website's pricing content, so the posts and the site always match.

To regenerate, run this with the local web and admin dev servers running (they provide the beer.krd screenshots):

```bash
pnpm --filter @beer/posts render
```

It writes to `marketing/posts/out/`:

- `NN-slug/`: the images for each post: carousel slides, single posts (4:5), and Reel covers and Stories (9:16);
- `captions.md`: every caption ready to copy and paste, plus the Reel shooting scripts;
- `index.html`: a review gallery of everything.
